Haiyue Song

宋海越 · LLM Engineer at Preferred Networks

haiyuesong at preferred.jp

I am an LLM engineer at Preferred Networks, working on post-training. Before that, I was a technical researcher at NICT, working on machine translation. I received my Ph.D. from Kyoto University in 2024, advised by Prof. Sadao Kurohashi and Prof. Chenhui Chu.

My research interests center on large language models, including agent harness, long-context, reinforcement learning, and continual pre-training. Previously, I worked on machine translation, especially in low-resource, multilingual, and multimodal scenarios.
